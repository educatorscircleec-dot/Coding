# Series 2: Math Hacks with EduCrew

**Audience:** ages 12 and up, from teens to adult learners (parents, career changers, anyone
refreshing their maths).
**Format:** 45-second vertical shorts. One trick per episode: hook → trick → *why* it works →
a real-life use → a challenge for the comments.
**Subject:** Mathematics, chosen because it's practical, evergreen and part of EduCrew's core.
**Look:** clean animated maths on a notebook-style board, numbers that move and swap, the
EduCrew logo, and the host sticker used sparingly (opening and sign-off only).
**Content rules:** no politics, no religion, no money advice beyond simple arithmetic, nothing
offensive. Every trick must be mathematically exact, and the "why" explained honestly.
**Free only:** Kokoro voice and locally rendered motion graphics (see CLAUDE.md).
