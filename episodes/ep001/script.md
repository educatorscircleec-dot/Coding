# EP001 — The Flickering Lantern

**COLD OPEN**

EXT. LANTERN LANE — DUSK
Lanterns blink on one by one down the lane. One sputters.

INT. PIP'S LANTERN WORKSHOP — CONTINUOUS
PIP rolls between shelves, humming, polishing lanterns.

**THE PROBLEM**

The door creaks. MRS. ALDER steps in, holding a lantern that flickers weakly.

MRS. ALDER
It's been sulking all week.

PIP
Sulking? Lanterns don't sulk! ...Do they?

**TRY & FAIL**

Pip tightens every bolt. The lantern flickers worse.

PIP
I can fix this. By myself. Definitely.

**THE INSIGHT**

Mrs. Alder sets down her basket and holds the lantern with Pip. It steadies, glowing brighter.

MRS. ALDER
Some things work better with two hands.

**FIX & PAYOFF / BUTTON**

The lantern blazes gold. Pip spins happily, and every lantern on the shelf lights up at once.
