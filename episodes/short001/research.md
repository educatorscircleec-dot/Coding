## Patterns that work
1. **Animals beat every other subject.** They took 3 of the top 5 spots.
2. **Question hooks.** "How do…?", "What if…?" and "Can you name all…?" titles are a promise the video has to pay off.
3. **One surprising fact every 4–5 s**, shown as a list of quick visual reveals.
4. **A challenge or comment prompt at the end** ("Can you name…", "Which one are you?").
5. **Real-science what-ifs** get a lot of likes from a small account, so there's room for newcomers.
