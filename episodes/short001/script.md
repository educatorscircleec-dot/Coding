**On-screen text (first 2 s):** HOW DO ANIMALS SLEEP? 😴

**Fact check:** each fact is simplified for kids, but true.
- Flamingos often rest on one leg.
- Bats roost upside down, gripping with their feet.
- Dolphins use *unihemispheric* sleep: one half of the brain rests while the other stays alert.
- Sea otters sometimes hold paws, or wrap themselves in kelp, so they don't drift apart while sleeping.
- Giraffes sleep in short bursts that add up to only a few hours a day.
- Badgers sleep in underground tunnel systems called setts.
