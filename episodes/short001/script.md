**Fact check** (simplified for kids, but accurate):
- Flamingos often rest on one leg. Saving body heat is the leading explanation, so the script says "may help".
- Most of the world's flamingos (mainly lesser flamingos) gather on the soda lakes of East Africa's Rift Valley.
- Bats roost upside down and take off by letting go and dropping into flight. Most bat species live in the tropics.
- Dolphins sleep with one brain half at a time (unihemispheric sleep), keeping one eye open.
- Sea otters hold paws or wrap themselves in kelp so they don't drift apart. About 90% of the world's sea otters live in coastal Alaska.
- Giraffes sleep in short naps, often only a few minutes long, and curl their neck back onto their body in deep sleep. They live mostly in East and Southern Africa.
