# MATH HACKS 001 — The Percent Flip (45 s, vertical, ages 12+ to adult learners)

| Board | Narration (free Kokoro voice) |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| 8% of 25 = ? | What's eight percent of twenty-five? Quick, no calculator! |
| 8% of 25 → FLIP IT! | Here's a secret most people never learn. Percentages can flip. |
| the 8 and 25 swap places → "same answer!" | Eight percent of twenty-five is the same as twenty-five percent of eight. |
| 25% of 8 = ¼ of 8 = 2 | And twenty-five percent is just a quarter. A quarter of eight is two! |
| percent = out of 100 | Why does it work? Percent simply means out of a hundred. |
| 8 × 25 ÷ 100 = 25 × 8 ÷ 100 = 2 | So both are eight times twenty-five, divided by a hundred. Same answer! |
| 4% of 75 → 75% of 4 = 3 | Try four percent of seventy-five. Flip it to seventy-five percent of four. That's three! |
| 12% of 50 → 50% of 12 = 6 | Leaving a twelve percent tip on fifty? Flip it. Half of twelve is six. |
| Friendly percents: 10% = ÷10, 25% = ÷4, 50% = ÷2 | Pro tip: flip it whenever one number is a friendly percent, like ten, twenty-five or fifty. |
| 18% of 50 = ? | Your turn. What's eighteen percent of fifty? Drop your answer in the comments! |

**Maths check:** 8% × 25 = 2 ✓ · 4% × 75 = 3 ✓ · 12% × 50 = 6 ✓ · comment challenge 18% × 50 = 9 ✓.
It works because a% of b = a × b ÷ 100, and multiplication can be done in any order.
