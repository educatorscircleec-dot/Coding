# EduCrew Biography Series — Character & Style Bible (locked in Episode 1)

Reuse these exact descriptions (and `character_reference.png`) in every episode. Faces are never shown.

## The girl (central character) — Episode 1 age: ~6
- Height ~112 cm, slim, small narrow shoulders, thin arms.
- Hair: long, thick, jet-black, two neat plaits tied with **red ribbons**; centre parting.
- Clothing: knee-length **yellow cotton frock with tiny white flower print**, puff sleeves; white ankle socks; brown buckle sandals (barefoot indoors).
- Silhouette: two plaits swinging behind her shoulders, frock flaring at the knee.

## Mother — early 30s
- Medium height, slender.
- **Pale green cotton salwar kameez**, **cream dupatta** over shoulders/loosely over head.
- Black hair in a low bun; **thin gold bangles** on both wrists.

## Father — mid 30s
- Tall, lean; short neat black hair.
- **Light blue half-sleeve cotton shirt** tucked into **dark grey trousers**; **steel wristwatch on left wrist**.

## Home & world
- Modest 1990s middle-class Indian home: lime-washed pale blue walls, red-oxide floor, steel utensils, wooden almirah, ceiling fan, window grilles, small sunlit courtyard with clothesline and potted tulsi/money plant, narrow residential lane outside a green iron gate.

## Look
- Photoreal 35mm live-action, natural sunlight, warm saturated 90s colour, gentle handheld/dolly moves.
- Framing: rear views, over-the-shoulder, hands, silhouettes, cropped bodies. No faces, no text, no music.
